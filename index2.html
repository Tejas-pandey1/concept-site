Our tiny manifesto / 001

We think everyday objects deserve a little theatre. So we treat each soda drop like a mini art release: a flavour with a point of view, packaging with a second life, and a batch small enough to feel special.

No fake scarcity theatre. We tell you what the run is, celebrate the people who collect, and let each bottle be its own little artifact.
